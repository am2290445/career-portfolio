# Statistical Programmer / Statistical Analyst

<span class="eyebrow">CAREER FILE 07 / STATISTICAL SOFTWARE</span>

## Job overview

A Statistical Programmer writes and validates code used to prepare datasets, perform statistical analyses, and produce reproducible outputs. Work may take place in healthcare research, pharmaceuticals, government, finance, market research, or other fields where reliable statistical evidence matters.

## Job description / responsibilities

- Prepare and validate data for analysis.
- Program statistical procedures and summaries.
- Produce tables, listings, figures, and reports.
- Check results for accuracy, reproducibility, and traceability.
- Document code, assumptions, and quality-control procedures.
- Collaborate with statisticians, researchers, analysts, or subject-matter experts.

## Skills and tools

Statistics, programming, data management, careful documentation, and quality assurance. SAS is used in some regulated research environments; R, Python, SQL, and other statistical tools appear in different sectors.

## Education and preparation

Statistics, mathematics, data science, computer science, health informatics, or related study may fit. Some roles require domain knowledge or advanced degrees; others emphasize programming and applied statistical skills.

## How it uses data analytics

The work makes statistical analysis reproducible and auditable. It supports descriptive summaries, statistical tests, model outputs, and structured reporting. In regulated contexts, validation and traceability are essential.

## How the role can help a business grow

- Improve confidence in evidence used for decisions.
- Reduce errors through repeatable, validated workflows.
- Support product, treatment, or process evaluation.
- Help organizations meet research, quality, or reporting obligations.
- Make complex findings easier for decision-makers to review.

**Illustrative business example:** A research organization needs consistent analysis outputs across study sites. A Statistical Programmer writes reusable programs, validates outputs, and documents the process. Researchers can review comparable evidence with a clearer audit trail.

## Portfolio project

Use a public, non-sensitive dataset. Create a reproducible analysis in R or Python with a defined question, data checks, statistical method, annotated output, and limitations. Avoid patient or confidential business information.

## Career development

Senior Statistical Programmer, Statistical Analyst, Biostatistical Programmer, Statistical Systems Specialist, or roles supporting data standards and research operations.
