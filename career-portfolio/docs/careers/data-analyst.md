# Data Analyst

<span class="eyebrow">CAREER FILE 01 / ANALYTICS</span>

## Job overview

A Data Analyst gathers, cleans, explores, and interprets data so teams can understand what is happening and make informed decisions. The role often works with business stakeholders to turn an unclear question into a measurable analysis.

## Job description / responsibilities

- Collect data from spreadsheets, databases, business applications, and other sources.
- Clean, validate, and organize data so the analysis is dependable.
- Use SQL, spreadsheets, and statistical methods to investigate patterns.
- Build reports, charts, and dashboards that communicate key performance indicators (KPIs).
- Explain findings to nontechnical partners and recommend next steps.
- Monitor business performance and investigate unusual changes.

## Skills and tools

SQL, Excel or Google Sheets, data cleaning, descriptive statistics, data visualization, communication, and critical thinking. Python or R, Power BI, Tableau, Looker, probability, and database concepts are often useful.

## Education and preparation

Employers commonly request a bachelor's degree in a quantitative, business, information systems, or related field, but requirements vary. Coursework in statistics, databases, programming, business analysis, and visualization can help.

## How it uses data analytics

The analyst applies descriptive and diagnostic analysis: what happened, where it happened, how it compares with expectations, and what factors may explain it. Some roles also use forecasting, cohorts, or experiments.

## How the role can help a business grow

- Identify products, channels, or customer segments associated with stronger sales.
- Find customer drop-off points and service problems.
- Reveal delays, rework, waste, and avoidable costs.
- Replace assumptions with shared metrics and evidence.
- Detect unusual activity or performance changes early.

**Illustrative business example:** An analyst studies an online shopping funnel and finds that customers abandon checkout most often at the shipping-cost step. The analyst quantifies the drop-off and compares it across customer groups. The business can test clearer shipping information or delivery options and measure whether completed purchases improve. The analysis informs a test; it does not guarantee growth.

## Portfolio project

Analyze a clearly labeled sample retail dataset. Clean it with Python or SQL, define KPIs, create a Power BI or Tableau dashboard, and write a one-page decision memo. Explain dataset limitations and never present synthetic data as real company results.

## Career development

Junior Data Analyst → Data Analyst → Senior Data Analyst → Analytics Lead, Analytics Manager, or a specialist path in product, finance, operations, or marketing analytics.
