[Original Wipro job description](https://careers.wipro.com/job/Junior-Forward-Deployed-Engineer/160017-en_US/)

**Location shown:** East Brunswick, New Jersey, plus other locations.

**Posting date:** October 26, 2026.

**Deadline:** Not stated in the posting.
**Availability:** The employer page was accessible when checked November 12, 2026. Confirm it is still open before applying.

### What the posting says

- Help deploy AI and digital tools in a client's work environment.
- Connect the tools to client systems, data, and workflows.
- Test and troubleshoot the setup.
- Support language-model applications, AI agents, prompts, and data retrieval.
- Work with clients and technical teams and document the work.

The posting asks for a recent bachelor's or master's degree in computer science, engineering, or a related technical field, a 3.0 GPA, and 0–2 years of relevant experience. It names programming, APIs, SQL or NoSQL databases, cloud basics, AI/ML basics, Git, and communication. It shows compensation of $65,000–$78,950.

### Why it matters to the research

This job shows that applied AI work includes connecting data and systems, testing, and helping people use a tool. It is not only model building.
