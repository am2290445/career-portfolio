## AI, business, and information systems in plain language

1. **Business asks a question:** for example, “How much stock should we order?”
2. **An information system organizes the facts:** sales, dates, products, and inventory are stored and made available to the team.
3. **AI helps with a task:** a model may estimate future demand or summarize patterns.
4. **A person checks the result:** is the data current, is the estimate reasonable, and could a mistake harm a customer or the business?
5. **The team measures the outcome:** did the decision reduce shortages, cost, or wasted work?

AI contributes when it helps people make a business process more useful, timely, or consistent. Information systems connect the AI tool to data, users, and everyday work. People remain responsible for reviewing accuracy, privacy, security, bias, and business value.

## Employer comparison: implementation and co-op work

### Wipro — Junior Forward Deployed Engineer

The Wipro posting describes an early-career, client-facing engineering role. A junior employee helps connect applied AI and digital tools to a client's software, data, and workflow; tests the setup; troubleshoots issues with guidance; and prepares documentation. The posting names Python, JavaScript, Java or similar programming, APIs, SQL or NoSQL, cloud exposure, AI/ML basics, Git, and communication. It requests a recent technical bachelor's or master's degree, a 3.0 GPA, and 0–2 years of experience.

### Church & Dwight — AI Developer Co-op

This nine-month graduate co-op focuses on developing and evaluating prototypes for real business needs. The student may work with Python, SQL, APIs, generative AI, retrieval-augmented generation, embeddings, vector databases, and evaluation methods. It also expects documentation and presentations to both technical and non-technical audiences. The source specifies enrollment in a related technical master's program; review the employer's full work-authorization requirements.

### What the comparison suggests

Both roles connect AI to business needs through information systems. Wipro emphasizes putting a solution into a client's existing environment. Church & Dwight emphasizes building and evaluating prototypes inside a company. Both call for data preparation, testing, clear documentation, and communication. These are observations from two postings, not a rule for every employer.
