# Analytics Engineer

<span class="eyebrow">CAREER FILE 04 / DATA MODELING</span>

## Job overview

An Analytics Engineer works between data engineering and data analysis. The role transforms raw or partially prepared data into clean, documented, reusable datasets that analysts and business teams can use consistently.

## Job description / responsibilities

- Transform data in a warehouse using SQL.
- Develop reusable data models and metric definitions.
- Build tests that check uniqueness, completeness, and valid relationships.
- Document datasets, assumptions, and lineage.
- Collaborate with data engineers and analysts to understand business logic.
- Manage changes through version control and review processes.

## Skills and tools

Advanced SQL, data modeling, Git, testing, documentation, and warehouse concepts. dbt is common in many analytics engineering environments; warehouses may include Snowflake, BigQuery, Redshift, or Databricks.

## Education and preparation

Information systems, analytics, computer science, or another quantitative/technical background may fit. Strong SQL and practical data modeling examples are especially useful.

## How it uses data analytics

The role prepares the semantic and analytical layer: consistent definitions of customers, revenue, products, time periods, and other business concepts. This reduces conflicting calculations across dashboards and analyses.

## How the role can help a business grow

- Reduce time spent cleaning and reconciling data.
- Help departments use the same definitions of performance.
- Make new analyses and dashboards faster to create.
- Improve confidence in metrics used for planning.
- Support scaling as data sources and business complexity increase.

**Illustrative business example:** Sales and finance teams calculate recurring revenue differently. An Analytics Engineer works with stakeholders to document the agreed definition, creates a tested data model, and makes it reusable. Leaders gain a clearer basis for comparing performance and planning.

## Portfolio project

Transform raw orders and customer tables into documented models. Include SQL, tests, metric definitions, Git history, and a visual lineage diagram.

## Career development

Senior Analytics Engineer, Data Engineer, Data Architect, or Analytics Engineering Lead.
