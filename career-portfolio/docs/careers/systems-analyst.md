# Systems Analyst / Business Systems Analyst

<span class="eyebrow">CAREER FILE 08 / INFORMATION SYSTEMS</span>

## Job overview

A Systems Analyst studies how an organization works and evaluates how information systems, software, and processes can better support its needs. The role connects business requirements with technical solutions.

## Job description / responsibilities

- Interview stakeholders and document business requirements.
- Map current workflows and identify pain points.
- Analyze how systems exchange and store information.
- Evaluate software options and recommend improvements.
- Translate needs into functional requirements and acceptance criteria.
- Coordinate testing, implementation, training, and change management.
- Monitor whether a solution meets business goals.

## Skills and tools

Requirements gathering, process mapping, SQL and database fundamentals, systems analysis, communication, documentation, testing, and project coordination. Tools may include BPMN, UML, Jira, Visio, and enterprise platforms.

## Education and preparation

Management Information Systems, Business and Information Systems, computer science, business, or related study can be relevant. Knowledge of business processes and technical concepts is important.

## How it uses data analytics

A Systems Analyst uses operational data, process metrics, user feedback, and system-performance information to understand the problem and evaluate a solution. Data helps establish a baseline and assess whether the change worked.

## How the role can help a business grow

- Reduce redundant or manual work.
- Improve information flow and accuracy.
- Help teams select systems that fit real requirements.
- Improve customer and employee experiences.
- Connect technology investments to measurable goals.

**Illustrative business example:** A company manually transfers order information between tools, causing delays and duplicate entry. A Systems Analyst maps the process, measures where errors occur, documents requirements, and helps evaluate an integration. After implementation, the team compares processing time and error rates with the baseline.

## Portfolio project

Document a sample business process, identify a measurable problem, create an as-is/to-be workflow, write requirements, propose a data or system solution, and define success measures.

## Career development

Senior Systems Analyst, Business Analyst, Product Analyst, Business Architect, IT Project Manager, or Business Systems Manager.
