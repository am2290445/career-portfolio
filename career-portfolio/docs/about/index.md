# About Aryanna

## The connection I am building

I am studying Business and Information Systems and am interested in how technology supports the way organizations operate, understand information, and make decisions. I have an Associate degree in Business Administration and want to continue building my technical foundation.

My interests include programming, databases, data analysis, and the systems that connect business needs with technology. I enjoy the combination of analytical thinking and creating something practical.

## My professional archetypes

### The Explorer — primary

Curiosity, discovery, learning, and asking better questions. This archetype shapes the way I investigate unfamiliar roles, compare tools, and look for connections between business challenges and technical solutions.

### The Creator — supporting

Experimentation, making, and translating ideas into something useful. This archetype shapes the way I approach projects, websites, dashboards, databases, and documentation.

## My emerging point of view

**I want to help organizations turn information into better decisions and useful systems.**
