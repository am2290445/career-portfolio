# Business Intelligence Analyst

<span class="eyebrow">CAREER FILE 02 / BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE</span>

## Job overview

A Business Intelligence (BI) Analyst helps an organization understand performance through trusted metrics, reporting systems, dashboards, and analysis. The role connects business questions with data definitions and decision-ready information.

## Job description / responsibilities

- Meet with departments to understand reporting and decision needs.
- Define and document KPIs, business rules, and metric definitions.
- Query, combine, and validate data from business systems.
- Build dashboards and recurring reports.
- Investigate changes in performance and explain what the data suggests.
- Help teams use consistent definitions and interpret reports accurately.

## Skills and tools

SQL, data modeling concepts, Excel, Power BI or Tableau, visualization, requirements gathering, communication, and business process knowledge. Python can help with automation and analysis.

## Education and preparation

Business, information systems, analytics, computer science, finance, or a related background may be relevant. Coursework in databases, statistics, and systems analysis can help. Employers set their own requirements.

## How it uses data analytics

BI work organizes descriptive and diagnostic analytics into repeatable decision tools. Dashboards track what changed, where results differ from targets, and which business unit or product may need attention.

## How the role can help a business grow

- Align teams around common performance measures.
- Find sales opportunities and underperforming products.
- Monitor customer retention and service quality.
- Reduce time spent assembling manual reports.
- Make operational issues visible sooner.

**Illustrative business example:** A retailer has different sales totals in marketing and finance reports because departments use different definitions. A BI Analyst documents a shared sales metric, validates its source, and publishes a dashboard. Leaders can compare performance consistently and spend less time reconciling reports.

## Portfolio project

Design a sales and operations dashboard using sample data. Include a metric dictionary, SQL queries, dashboard screenshots, and a short explanation of decisions the dashboard could support.

## Career development

Senior BI Analyst, BI Developer, Analytics Engineer, BI Manager, or Analytics Manager.
