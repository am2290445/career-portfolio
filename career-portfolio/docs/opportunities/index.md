## Other job titles to search

- **Data analyst intern:** organize information, find patterns, and explain results.
- **Business systems intern:** learn how software supports work and help improve a process.
- **AI or data co-op:** work for a longer term on data and AI projects.
- **AI business analyst:** connect business needs with system requirements and possible AI uses.
- **AI solutions associate:** help set up AI tools and connect them to business data.
- **Automation analyst:** find repeated steps and help test safe ways to automate them.

## How skills connect

| Skill | What it helps with |
|---|---|
| Business knowledge | Understand the problem a team wants to solve. |
| Data analysis | Find patterns and support decisions. |
| Databases and SQL | Organize, find, and check information. |
| Systems analysis | Connect user needs, business steps, data, and software. |
| Programming | Build or test a tool or automate a task. |
| Communication | Explain a result and its limits to different people. |

Use coursework and projects as evidence for skills. Do not list a tool as a strength without an example of using it.

## A simple example

A store wants to know which products may sell more next month. Its information system holds past sales. An analyst checks the data. An AI or statistics tool estimates demand. A worker reviews the result before ordering. The business checks whether this reduced shortages or waste.

AI can help a business, but people still need to check data quality, privacy, accuracy, and whether the result is useful.
