# AI, business, and information systems

## Research question

**What internships, co-ops, and early-career jobs connect AI, business, and information systems, and what skills do employers ask applicants to use?**

## The simple connection

- **Business** is the work an organization does and the goals it wants to reach.
- **Information systems (IS)** connect people, processes, data, and software so work can happen.
- **Artificial intelligence (AI)** can help find patterns, make predictions, sort information, or create a first draft.

AI is useful when it helps with a real need and people can check the result.

## A simple example

A store wants to know how much of each product to order. Its sales system holds past orders. An analyst checks the data. A model estimates what may sell next month. A worker reviews the forecast and makes the order. The business later checks whether the plan reduced shortages or waste.

The information system supplies and organizes data. AI helps with a prediction. A person checks the result and makes the decision.

## Research method

I reviewed employer job descriptions and program pages on November 12, 2026. I recorded each role's location, work, skills, stated qualifications, pay if shown, deadline wording, and whether the employer listing could still be reached. The [opportunity page](/opportunities/) separates listings that appeared available from postings whose listed deadlines had passed. The [employer comparison](/careers/employer-comparison) compares Wipro and MITRE.

### What the sources say

- JPMorganChase's 2027 Data & AI internship page describes building data and AI solutions tied to business outcomes. It names Python, SQL, data management, governance, and communication, and shows a November 20, 2026 deadline for the listed U.S. program.
- Wipro's Junior Forward Deployed Engineer posting describes helping clients connect AI tools with their systems, data, and workflows. It lists programming, APIs, databases, cloud basics, AI/ML basics, Git, and communication.
- Church & Dwight's AI Developer Co-op describes building and testing AI prototypes for business needs. It lists a related technical master's program as a requirement.
- Axtria's Campus 2027 AI & Data Analyst listing describes AI and data work for life-sciences clients and gives a graduation window and skills.
- Johnson & Johnson's IDAR internship postings describe data, systems, analytics, process, and AI work in clinical development. Their listed November 8 and October 11 deadlines had passed by this review date, so they are shown as recent examples, not open jobs.
- The MITRE Associate AI Solutions Architect listing describes AI-enabled applications and workflows. Its official job page could not be opened during this review, so I cannot confirm whether that job is still available.

These are facts about individual postings. They do not prove that every job with a similar title has the same duties or requirements.

### What I infer

The postings suggest a connection between AI and information systems: employers need people who can work with data and software, understand a business or user need, test a solution, and explain it to others. This is an interpretation of the postings, not a promise that a particular skill will guarantee a job.

## How AI can help a business

AI may help with customer service, planning, repeated tasks, reporting, or spotting unusual patterns. Information systems connect the tool to the right data and work process. People still need to check accuracy, privacy, security, bias, and business value.
