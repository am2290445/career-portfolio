# Skills for AI, business, and information systems

This page connects study and practice to the work found in AI and information systems roles. Skills are strongest when they have an example to show.

## Three areas working together

| Area | In simple words | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Business** | Understand the goal or problem. | Explain what a team needs before choosing a tool. |
| **Information systems** | Connect people, processes, data, and software. | Map how information moves through an order process. |
| **AI and data** | Use information to find patterns, make a prediction, or draft a response. | Check an AI summary against the original data. |

## Skills employers ask for

- **Data:** SQL, spreadsheets, data cleaning, charts, and data quality.
- **Technology:** Python or another programming language, APIs, databases, and basic cloud services.
- **AI:** basic AI/ML ideas, prompt workflows, testing, and responsible use.
- **Systems:** requirements, business processes, testing, and documentation.
- **People skills:** writing clearly, working with a team, asking questions, and presenting results.

The job listings on this site do not all ask for the same skills. Compare a posting to your own experience and choose examples that are true.

## Show a skill with a small project

1. Choose a simple business question.
2. Use public or sample data, not private customer information.
3. Clean the data and explain what it contains.
4. Use a spreadsheet, SQL, or Python to find a pattern.
5. Create a chart or short summary.
6. If AI is used, check its answer against the data and explain what a person should review.
7. Write a README with the question, steps, result, and limits.

This gives an employer evidence of how business thinking, systems, data, and technology can work together.
